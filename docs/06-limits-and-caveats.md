# Limits and caveats

[Home](../README.md) | Previous: [What we found](05-what-we-found.md) | Next: [How it was made](07-how-it-was-made.md)

Please read this page before relying on anything in the repo.

## This is not advice

This is research written for learning and discussion. It does not consider your finances, goals, tax position or risk tolerance. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Shares can fall to zero. Cheap shares can stay cheap or get cheaper.

## What was NOT done

- **Most flagged companies were not examined.** The screening tools flagged 150 US, 78 Australian and 122 Indian names. Only a few dozen were checked in depth, and not all of those checks used the company's own filings. We do not have the full lists.
- **It is not a complete survey of any market.** Free tools showed only part of the Indian list (about 20 of 158 rows). The Australian light check used about 21 names from one website.
- **No new data refresh for this repo.** The repo reorganises and explains work done on 13 October 2026.
- **No ten-year history was fully verified** for most companies, although the method calls for it.
- **No portfolio advice.** Taxes, brokerage costs, exchange rates, trading volume and suitability are outside this research.

## Where the numbers can be wrong

| Issue | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Vendor data errors** | Screening tools had wrong or stale ratios for several names. We recalculated from filings where we could; for some companies we could only check against other data websites. |
| **Stale or untimed prices** | Australian prices from a data feed carry no time stamp. US prices are from the 12 October close where checked. Anything else could be out of date. |
| **Some sources are not official** | A number of balance sheets came from data websites, not the company's own filing. These are flagged in the technical notes. |
| **Accounting is not cash** | Book value, inventory, receivables and investments can be worth less than their recorded values. |
| **Categories can mislead** | Some companies carried the wrong industry tag in the screen (for example OMH and PSEC). |
| **Currencies are not mixed** | Ratios are compared within each market. They are not converted into one portfolio. |
| **Small companies, thin trading** | Some names are very small. Shares may be hard to buy or sell at a fair price. |

## Specific warnings carried through from the research

- The vendor ratio for BOOM was wrong.
- For CAAS, outside owners' claims affect the net-net test.
- India's 122 count uses price to book, not price to tangible book.
- No full 150 / 78 / 122 lists were retained. Only leading rows were.
- Most stories end in "needs more homework". That is the finding, not a gap in the writing.

## How we handle disagreements between sources

1. A company's own official filing beats a data website.
2. A dated source beats an undated one.
3. A later filing beats an earlier one, and we say which we used.
4. If we cannot tell, we say "unverified" and do not guess.

## Privacy

The repo contains no passwords, access tokens, email addresses or personal information. Account and setup details were removed before publication. If you spot anything that should not be here, please open an issue.

## Using this responsibly

If you want to follow up on a name:

1. Look up the latest price yourself.
2. Read the company's most recent annual and half-year reports.
3. Recalculate the key ratios with the formulas in [How to read the numbers](04-reading-the-numbers.md).
4. Consider talking to a licensed adviser.
