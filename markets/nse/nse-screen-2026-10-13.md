# NSE Schloss-style asset screen
Research snapshot: 13 October 2026. INR; financial amounts in crore rupees unless stated. Quotes below use the completed 12 October session, not the ongoing 13 October session.

## Answer
DEN Networks is the strongest net-net research candidate. GSFC and Maithan Alloys pass a strict tangible-book screen using their audited March 2026 balance sheets. None is an unconditional verified buy: DEN has results expected today, GSFC's June liabilities rose materially and September results have been delayed, and Maithan's assets include a large investment portfolio requiring separate valuation. These are a prioritized diligence list, not personal financial advice or evidence that the whole NSE lacks other candidates.

## Calculations
Use consolidated common equity = share capital + other equity, excluding non-controlling interests. Tangible common equity subtracts goodwill and other intangible assets. Right-of-use assets are retained; lease liabilities are included in the conservative leverage column. NCAV subtracts ALL recorded liabilities, not merely debt. Annual financial statements are dated 31 March 2026; June 2026 results reviewed separately.

### DEN Networks
Annual report printed pages 126-127: equity attributable to owners Rs3,784.808cr; goodwill Rs149.583cr; other intangible assets Rs3.047cr; current assets Rs3,546.701cr; ALL liabilities Rs457.227cr. TCE = 3,784.808 - 149.583 - 3.047 = 3,632.178. NCAV = 3,546.701 - 457.227 = 3,089.474.

Shares: 477,223,845 issued; EPS weighted average 476,765,914 excludes 457,931 employee-trust shares. Use the higher issued count conservatively for market cap; this corrects the initial quick estimate. No EPS dilution is shown in FY26 or June results.

Cash/asset quality: cash and equivalents Rs19.96cr, other bank balances Rs3,118.714cr, current investments Rs143.866cr. Do NOT call all of this unrestricted cash: Rs177.536cr bank balances are margin/security accounts. The report says Rs2,928.315cr deposits with maturities over 12 months can be withdrawn without prior notice or penalty on principal. Equity also includes Rs121.926cr deferred-tax assets. Removing those from TCE gives P/TB about 0.344, still cheap.

Stress test: subtract minority interests Rs41.289cr, all earmarked bank balances Rs177.536cr, all disclosed contingent liabilities Rs94.239cr and capital commitments Rs10.094cr from NCAV. 2/3 stressed NCAV remains about Rs1,844.21cr, above Rs1,209.29cr market cap. This deliberately over-conservative test is not a liquidation forecast.

Why cheap, evidenced rather than guessed: FY26 sales Rs974.28cr vs Rs1,005.41cr; other income Rs232.227cr exceeds PBT Rs208.115cr. FY26 CFO is negative Rs16.593cr and capex Rs41.042cr. Cash is not being returned: no FY26 dividend, and Rs2,045cr raised by a 2018-19 preferential allotment remains invested pending use. Promoters hold 74.90%. June-quarter revenue Rs242.774cr; PAT Rs34.589cr vs Rs53.640cr a year earlier. The company presentation reports zero gross debt and broadly defined cash/investments Rs3,383cr as of June.

Verdict: best net-net diligence candidate, but wait for/recheck the September results expected at the 13 October board meeting before calling the March balance sheet current. No return of cash or liquidation is assured.

### GSFC
Annual report printed page 198: common equity 79.70 + 12,186.75 = Rs12,266.45cr; other intangible assets Rs4.24cr, no goodwill balance. Current assets Rs6,094.04cr; liabilities 850.92 + 1,261.11 = Rs2,112.03cr. Borrowings Rs25cr; leases Rs1.66cr. FY26 diluted shares 398,477,530, same as basic; no dilution effect. Established 1962.

Asset quality: non-current investments Rs4,609.70cr include associates; government subsidy receivables Rs1,452.88cr; cash Rs191.77cr plus other bank balances Rs8.34cr. Low reported leverage is not the same as liquid asset backing. FY26 CFO Rs136.14cr vs capex Rs307.78cr, so CFO minus capex is negative Rs171.64cr. Disclosed claims include Rs1,332.50cr in the 'Other' category plus tax claims; labour claims are not ascertainable. These require investigation.

Latest filing check: June-quarter consolidated sales Rs3,583.15cr, owners' PAT Rs158.53cr. Segment totals give assets Rs16,633.75cr and liabilities Rs3,575.36cr, up from March liabilities Rs2,112.03cr. No June detailed debt/cash/intangible balance sheet in the three-page result; do not carry March debt ratios forward as current. June OCI is substantial and investment values move with markets.

Critical recency correction: a stale quote-page news item said results would be considered on Oct12. The company's Oct6 primary notice explicitly CANCELS the Oct12 board meeting for September half-year results, with revised date to follow. Official notice, not stale news, governs.

Verdict: strict tangible-book candidate on audited data, watch pending fresh balance-sheet/debt verification and contingent-claim review. Not a net-net.

### Maithan Alloys
Use the REVISED consolidated annual financial statements approved Aug4 following the Impex merger, not unrevised numbers. Printed page 172: common equity 29.11 + 4,116.85 = Rs4,145.96cr; goodwill Rs16.34cr; intangibles Rs0.29cr; current assets Rs3,331.35cr; total liabilities Rs735.19cr; borrowings Rs235.30cr and leases Rs1.90cr. Diluted shares 29,111,550, no potential shares. Incorporated 1985.

Asset quality: current investments Rs1,653.12cr and non-current investments Rs1,187.24cr; cash Rs58.96cr and other bank balances Rs47.85cr. The investment notes include quoted/unquoted equities, mutual funds, alternative funds and portfolio managers. Do NOT classify it as net cash by adding all investments to cash. Inventories Rs1,246.54cr include assets needing commodity/land-quality review. Only Rs106.81cr cash plus bank balances is less than Rs237.20cr borrowing-plus-leases.

FY26 CFO negative Rs9.86cr; capex Rs24.88cr. Contingent liabilities Rs40.01cr and investment commitments Rs313.13cr. FY26 owners' PAT Rs433.65cr vs Rs630.18cr. June sales Rs539.53cr vs Rs632.31cr; other income Rs417.17cr, owners' PAT Rs393.04cr. Profit is heavily exposed to investments rather than solely operating ferro-alloy earnings. June segment liabilities Rs1,057.08cr vs March Rs735.19cr; full June debt/cash breakdown is not published here. September announcements show further investments in ESDS and One97 Communications; ownership/investment portfolio must be revalued, not extrapolated.

Verdict: strict tangible-book candidate on audited data, but separately value its investment portfolio and subsequent transactions before a buy verdict. Not a net-net and not a cash box. This is a ferro-alloy operating company rather than a pure miner, but commodity cyclicality still matters.

## Coverage and exclusions
Discovery coverage: Downstox claims 3,205 NSE stocks with fundamentals, 697 below book, refreshed 11 Oct 2026; only its top 50 rows are publicly shown. This is an aggregator's claimed universe, not an audited NSE census. A Screener low-P/B, low-debt screen's first 25 rows were also inspected. Primary filing diligence covered the three shortlisted operating companies above. No exhaustive all-NSE rank or count of true tangible-book/net-net passes is claimed.

Excluded from this simple operating-asset screen: financials, investment holding companies, insurers, REITs/InvITs and miners, which need separate asset-quality work. Several discovery rows are financial or holding companies (e.g. Nalwa Sons, Summit Securities, BF Investment, Kalyani Investment), so their low P/B is not an automatic pass. Tiny-cap/SME and distressed names shown by discovery remain unverified, not buys. Mere P/B<1 without goodwill/intangible checks is WATCH ONLY.

Rejected discovery shortcut: the Screener page titled 'Net net scanner Benjamin Graham' has a DIFFERENT formula: half the price compared with cash + 75% receivables + 50% inventory - debt. It subtracts debt rather than all liabilities and is not this task's net-net screen. Its claimed 3,751 matches were not used as net-net evidence.
