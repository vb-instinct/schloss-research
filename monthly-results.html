What changes next

The scheduled monthly run adds a new month and compares it with the prior snapshot. When new filings, corrected data or corporate actions are checked between runs, the affected row is revised with a dated change note and a source, and the previous snapshot is kept. New information is not automatically incorporated merely because it has been published elsewhere.

There is no previous month to compare in this first edition. "Not established" or "unverified" means we cannot certify the test; it does not mean a company failed or has a problem.

Source notes: Discovery counts, US diligence, NSE audited calculations, NSE follow-up, ASX filing diligence. Source files also remain available in the repository.

Quotes and balance sheets have different dates. ASX ratios are indicative because prices lack session timestamps. Recheck before relying on any number.
